About

I'm Francisco Biancardi, an Argentinian Senior VFX Compositor based in Buenos Aires. I've worked with studios like MPC and Framestore on shows such as House of the Dragon, Foundation, Silo and The Eternaut, and on features like Dark Phoenix and The Call of the Wild.

My first big step in the film industry was Metegol, Juan José Campanella's animated feature and the most ambitious animated film ever made in Argentina: a demanding stereoscopic pipeline built in Nuke with a brilliant local team. Since then I've worked with more than 24 studios, which taught me to adapt quickly to different pipelines and find new ways to solve each shot.

I also work as a VFX Supervisor, a role I first took on for a Netflix documentary series. I write my own Python tools for Nuke, and I keep training in color science, ACES, HDR and DIT workflows. I'm always looking for new challenges and talented people to make great images with.
